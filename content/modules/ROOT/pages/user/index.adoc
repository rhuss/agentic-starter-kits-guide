Welcome.
This deployment runs an OpenClaw agent for you, and you talk to it from your browser.

== What you can do here

OpenClaw's Control UI is a web app that the gateway serves, so it opens right in your browser and there's nothing to install.
From it you chat with the agent and follow the work it does.
Your administrator sets up the deployment, and you connect to it as a user.

== What to ask your administrator for

Before you can connect, ask the person who set up the deployment for three things.

The web address of the Control UI::
This is the link you open in your browser to reach the agent.

The gateway token::
You paste this token when the Control UI asks you to connect, and it's what lets you in.

Approval for your browser::
When a browser that is not on the cluster connects, the gateway asks for that connection to be approved.
Your administrator approves it, and then you reach the chat.

Once you have the address, paste the token, and your browser is approved, the chat interface is ready to use.
